# Privacy Policy for Brick Blast

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Brick Blast ("the game") is an Android game published by Chukwuemmanuel Daniel. This policy explains what information the game uses.

## Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location ourselves.

The game saves your progress (score, coins, settings, and level progress) only on your own device. We do not receive or store this data on any server.

## Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can learn how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads).

## Consent

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice later in the game's settings, where this option is available.

## Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
